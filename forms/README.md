# Forms

Official forms the coach reads locally to ask their questions word for word. They are not
committed: download them yourself.

- `parq-plus.pdf`: the PAR-Q+ from https://eparmedx.com (search "PAR-Q+"), unmodified.
  `parq-plus-es.pdf`, if present, is the official Spanish version from the same site and is
  used for Spanish-speaking clients. Without either file, /setup sends the client the link.
  The Spanish form is the 2018-based translation and the English one is the 2025 version. Both
  are official; use the client's language.
