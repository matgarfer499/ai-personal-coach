# Forms

The PAR-Q+ is the PAR-Q+ Collaboration's copyrighted form (https://eparmedx.com). Its terms
(Terms and Conditions, checked 2026-11-08) allow using the official, unmodified form without
charge, but require prior written permission to put its questions into electronic surveys,
apps or websites, to translate or adapt it, or to redistribute it. So:

- The form is never committed here and the coach never reads, quotes or translates its
  questions. `forms/*.pdf` stays in `.gitignore`.
- Rewording its questions would still be an adaptation, so the coach does not screen with it.
  The default entry screen is the ACSM 2015 preparticipation algorithm (recovery-habits.md
  "ACSM 2015 screening algorithm"): a published method, not a form, asked in the coach's own
  words (regular exercise, known cardiovascular, metabolic or renal disease, warning symptoms).
- The PAR-Q+ is an optional extra. /setup offers the official link in the client's language (the
  Collaboration's Spanish translation, or the current English form) and, if the client fills it
  in, asks only the two outcomes in its own words: any YES on the general questions, any YES on
  the follow-up questions. Those answers still apply, and the stricter outcome wins.
- Asking the questions in the chat would need written permission from the Collaboration
  (eparmedx@gmail.com).
