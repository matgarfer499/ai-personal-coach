## Dropped claims

4 corrections and drops, appended to `docs/dropped-claims.md` as D248 to D251. Main points: the RPE 15-17
figure was an instruction and the 86-89% HRmax was a whole boxing circuit; "multi-joint" and punch rate were

## Limits

- Pilot n=12 (Cheema) and 10 men (Scott); no long-term bag-only trial.
- Wilke's confidence intervals against moderate training and HIIT are wide; PEDro mean 5.9.
- Compendium page text could not show which codes are estimated (shown in red) or define "b/min".
