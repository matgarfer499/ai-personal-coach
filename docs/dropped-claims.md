# Dropped claims

Claims considered during research on 2026-10-24 that did not make it into the knowledge base, with the reason. Kept for transparency and as a to-do list: if a source turns up that supports one of these, it can be re-researched.

`research` = discarded by the researcher (source could not be opened or did not support it). `verify` = rejected by an independent checker. Exception: the four `verify` entries under "Intensity zone thresholds" (D132-D135 below) read as confirmations because they were lost to malformed checker output in Phase 1, not rejected.

## Gap round (2026-10-24)

Every entry below now ends with a triage group, assigned by hand:
- **(a) refuted or myth** (30 entries): the evidence contradicts it, the source was retracted or flagged, or it is a popular claim with no support. Stays dropped.
- **(b) low value for an ordinary-adult coach** (147): already covered by another rule, outside the target population, too fragile, or no coaching consequence. Stays dropped.
- **(c) useful, unconfirmed** (52): worth having if a source can be opened. 40 of them were retried, chosen for what the commands (/setup, /plan, /review, /menu) need; the other 12 are marked "not retried (40-claim cap)".

Retry outcomes (40 entries): 23 recovered, 2 partly recovered, 1 resolved as a negative finding (D063: even protein distribution does not improve body composition), 14 still dropped. Entries are numbered D001-D229 in order of appearance (strength D001-D052, nutrition D053-D114, cardio D115-D164, recovery D165-D229).
